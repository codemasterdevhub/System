Our Instructors

Troy Hunt

Cybersecurity Educator & Microsoft Regional Director

Troy is the creator of Have I Been Pwned, a widely used tool for checking compromised credentials. He teaches developers how to secure web applications using HTTPS, strong authentication, and proper security headers through his blog and online courses.

Bruce Schneier

Cryptography Expert & Public Policy Instructor

A renowned figure in cybersecurity, Bruce has authored several books on cryptography and web security. He teaches at Harvard and advises on global cyber policy, with a strong focus on best practices in secure systems and communication protocols.

Dr. Angela Yu

Full-Stack Developer & Online Instructor

Known for her top-rated web development courses on Udemy, Dr. Yu emphasizes building secure applications. Her teachings include HTTPS implementation, secure authentication, and backend protections using Node.js.

Kevin Mitnick

Ethical Hacker & Cybersecurity Consultant

Once a notorious hacker, Kevin became a trusted security advisor and instructor. He educated professionals on real-world threats, social engineering, and penetration testing, making complex web security concepts accessible.
